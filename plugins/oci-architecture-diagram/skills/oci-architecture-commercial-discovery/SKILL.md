# OCI Architecture Commercial Discovery

Read .source evidence and preserve facts, user statements, assumptions and recommendations separately. Record business objective, scope, environments, region/currency, workload ranges and exclusions in .oci-bom/cases/<case-slug>/.

Return missing answers only when they can change a service, resilience topology, region, model or pricing order of magnitude. State the low/base/high fallback and its cost impact. Do not put customer evidence in plugin assets, fixtures or shared knowledge.
